Common questions

Do I need to be home?

No. Most customers leave a key or a door code with us, stored encrypted and accessible only to your assigned crew.

Do you bring supplies?

Yes, everything including a vacuum. If you'd rather we use your products, leave them out and tell us in the booking notes.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us within 24 hours and we come back and redo the areas you flag, free. If it still isn't right, we refund the visit.

Is tipping expected?

Never expected, always passed on in full. Our cleaners start at $24 an hour before tips.

We started with two people and one van.

Clean Bee began in Kaysville in 2023 when Rosa Ibarra, after eleven years cleaning for other companies, got tired of watching good cleaners get paid badly and rotated between houses they'd never see twice.

So the company was built the other way around: cleaners are employees, not contractors, they start at $24 an hour, and they keep the same customers. It turns out people notice when the same two people have known their home for a year.
